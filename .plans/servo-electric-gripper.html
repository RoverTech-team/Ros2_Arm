1. Objective

Replace the arm's bare cylinder tip (j5 + tool0) with a mechanical impactive parallel gripper, servo-electric actuated (electric servo motor + leadscrew/rack translating two mirrored jaws), integrated end-to-end: URDF, ros2_control (Gazebo + mock), controllers, MoveIt/SRDF, launch, and the panel-interaction demo (grasp-based knob interaction).

3. Design decisions (confirmed with the user)

DecisionChoiceRationale
MountingKeep j5 wrist roll; gripper mounts on j5, replacing the j5 tip + tool0Wrist roll is essential to orient the jaws toward the panel knobs.
Jaw model2 mirrored prismatic joints (gripper_left_jaw_joint / gripper_right_jaw_joint)Standard sim parallel gripper; controlled by a joint_trajectory_controller over both jaws (Humble-native); servo-electric = leadscrew translating both jaws (kinematic model + effort/velocity caps).
Controljoint_trajectory_controller/JointTrajectoryController over both jaw jointsHumble ships no multi-joint GripperCommand controller (GripperActionController is single-DOF only, GripperCommandController arrived in newer releases); JTC over the 2 jaw joints works identically in Gazebo and mock; the demo drives it via a FollowJointTrajectory action client.
Stroke40 mm opening (±20 mm per jaw)Envelopes power knobs Ø32, rotary knobs Ø24, lever tips 8×6 mm → grasp-and-rotate for ALL knobs; no friction drags needed.

  • Grasp poses replace push poses in the element table; the IK/clearance probe (E1 of the interaction plan) is re-run with gripper geometry (jaws open, jaw volume accounted).

10. Blocker analysis

G1 — IK reachability with the longer end effector HIGH

What: tool0 moves from 40 mm to 95 mm beyond j5; the "grasp-from-above" pose for rotary knobs (tool0 z vertical over the knob row at x≈1.475, z≈0.32) and grasp-over-the-tip poses for levers must be reachable with 4 DOF.

Impact: Some grasp poses may be infeasible (tip pointing down at 1.5 m reach).

Mitigation: Re-run the IK probe (interaction plan E1) with the gripper model before writing demo code; per-element fallback chain (grasp from front vs above; pinch + swing instead of wrist-spin).

Residual risk: MEDIUM — fallbacks exist for every knob, verified in E2.

G2 — Self-collision & planning scene with jaws MEDIUM

What: The jaws are new collision bodies in OMPL planning (open state during approach — the planner sees the robot_description state ✓). Adjacent-link pairs must be disabled (SRDF diff §8); jaws vs panel/knobs must not be disabled (they must avoid the panel while moving).

Impact: Planning failures or illegal grazing if pairs are wrong.

Mitigation: SRDF pairs per §8; E1 probe samples approach paths with jaws open; verify no jaw-panel contact during approaches in E2/E3.

G3 — Grasp force vs knob resistance MEDIUM

What: Lever detent (0.4 N·m/rad → 0.63 N·m max ≈ 18 N at the 35 mm tip) and rotary friction (0.05/0.08 N·m) must be beaten while the grasp holds (pinch 40–60 N × μ ≈ 1.0–1.5 → 40–90 N tangential ✓ per §4 budget).

Impact: If the pad μ is low or the pinch force is capped by physics, the knob slips out mid-swing.

Verification experiment (E2): grasp-flip one lever and grasp-spin one rotary; if slip: add pad surface μ (1.5) in the xacro and/or raise effort to 40 N.

G4 — Prismatic joints through gz_ros2_control/GazeboSimSystem MEDIUM

What: The jaw joints are new prismatic DOF in the same ros2_control block driven by the gz plugin; URDF <limit effort> caps the physics force; the JTC-style position commands must convert cleanly (URDF → SDF conversion of prismatic joints is standard ✓).

Impact: Controller/hardware mismatch symptoms (NaN, unresponsive jaws) if interfaces are misdeclared.

Verification experiment (V2): after build, gz sdf -p the URDF and ros2 action send_goal /gripper_controller/follow_joint_trajectory (positions 0.02) in both modes; confirm jaw positions in /joint_states and physics motion in the GUI.

G5 — Mock parity LOW

What: mock_components/GenericSystem supports prismatic ✓; the gripper_controller must be spawned in the mock branch too (launch diff §7) and the demo's interact flag (mock = execution-only) still applies.

Impact: None if the spawner + config diffs land in both branches.

G6 — tool0 redefinition ripple MEDIUM

What: tool0 semantics change (grasp plane vs tip face); the interaction plan's pose math, the 40 mm rule, and any pose references in the demo/README must be updated coherently.

Impact: Inconsistent poses (off-by-55 mm mistakes) if done piecemeal.

Mitigation: Single source of truth: a GRIPPER_TOOL0_OFFSET constant + per-element grasp table generated by the probe; both plans' documents cross-referenced.

G7 — Servo-electric fidelity LOW

What: v1 models the servo as a position-commanded prismatic joint with force/velocity caps (kinematic). No torque/force-control loop, no current limits, no servo PID.

Impact: None for the demo; grasp force is only implicitly controlled (joint effort caps).

Future work: add an effort command interface + force feedback via GripperCommand max_effort if pinch-force control is ever needed.

G8 — Visual meshes LOW

What: Primitives for v1 (consistent with j4/j5); DAE files for the housing/jaws later.
